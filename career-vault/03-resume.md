## Things I will NOT put on this resume

The discipline part. Some things look impressive but signal weakness:

- "Proficient in 47 tools." Recruiters know nobody is proficient in 47 tools. Pick 5.
- "Team player." Empty. Replace with a specific instance of teamwork in a bullet.
- "Detail-oriented." Same.
- Self-rated skill bars (Figma: 4/5 stars). Reads as juvenile in 2026.
- Photo. BD norms differ. Default to no photo unless the role explicitly asks.
- Personal interests unless they tie to your work (e.g., "I redesign BD government forms for fun": yes; "I love hiking": no).
