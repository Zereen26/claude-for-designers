## Project overview

- **Project name:** "Oshudh Shesh" — The Arogga Prescription Refill Reminder
- **Team:** Spacing (Batch 8)
- **Start date:** 26 July 2026

## Problem definition

Chronic medication management for elderly patients whose remote caregivers struggle with manual
tracking. Research participant: "medicine supply tracking relies on memory and phone calls,
which leads to skipped doses and emergency orders."

### Key research findings

**User pain points:**
- 73.7% frequently forget to reorder before supplies deplete
- Manual pill counting creates significant mental stress
- Remote caregivers feel guilt about distance-related delays
- Elderly users find app navigation complex
- 100% confirmed managing distant family medications is challenging

**User receptivity:**
- 89.5% want automated refill reminders
- 63.2% would use one-click reorder carts
- Users demand confirmation controls before automatic orders

## Solution framework

**Primary persona:** Atique, 29 — remote businessman managing elderly parents' medications from
another city. Wants automated tracking to remove manual oversight under work pressure.

**Nine core features:**
1. Predictive refill reminders (3-4 days advance notice)
2. Digital pill box dashboard
3. One-click automated cart refill
4. Direct support team call system
5. Multi-patient profile management
6. Flexible partial purchase options
7. Prescription update workflow
8. Generic alternative suggestions
9. Medication adherence tracking for caregivers

## Success metrics

- Task success rate: target 6 of 6 users
- Reorder completion time: under 30 seconds
- Prediction accuracy: 5 of 6 user confirmations
