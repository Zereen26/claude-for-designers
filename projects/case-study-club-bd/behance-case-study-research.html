12-ta Case Study-r Modhe Common Pattern

Full re-read-er por pattern-gulo aro shorshoto hoyeche. Ei ta-i "Nayem bhai style" case study-r formula.

1Hero-e generic na, specific angle
Pathao-e "pre-book rides" (generic "ride app" na), Safe Ride Alert-e "women & students" (generic "commuters" na), ShareTrip-e "Optimizing The Flight Searching Experience" (generic "flight app" na).
2Problem ↔ Solution — alada, paired card, number diye
ShareTrip-e explicit "The Problem 1 / The Solution", "The Problem 2 / The Solution" sticky-note pair. Travela-te "Problem 01/02" numbered card. Unimart-e protita page-r (Home/Listing/Details) nijer Problem-Solution pair.
3Evidence — review, survey, audit, competitor table
ShareTrip-e real Trustpilot/Google review screenshot ("Such a stupid service" — 1-star). Travela-te real Play Store 2-star quote. HireMate-e 5-competitor feature table. SWOT analysis (ShareTrip-e 4 competitor). Assumption na, real proof.
4Process transparency — timeline, day/week-count
ShareTrip-e "01/18" theke "18/18" explicit page-counter + "5 Weeks" timeline. HireMate-e Strategy/Discovery/Solution day-count. Electricity Kiosk-e 4-step research process. Eta prove kore kaj structured, random na.
5Sketch → Mid-fi → Hi-fi — visible progression
Prottekta case study-e hand-drawn sketch dekhano hoy (na hoyle audit-type case study-e heuristic annotation), tarpor mid-fi, tarpor hi-fi. Kono case study sudhu final screen dekhay na — process dekhay.
6Local context, brand collateral, outcome, team — shesh-e
bKash (Travela), Bangla billboard (ShareTrip, Electricity Kiosk, Travela), real BD landmark. Prottekta-r shesh-e outcome stat ba impact line, tarpor team credit + mentor thanks (ekhon confirm hoyeche: Nayem Islam nijeo AROAM-e credited team member hisebe).

03 / 20 — Problem & Solution
Problem & Solution
Problem
Real Aroggo app-e "Patients" ar "Prescriptions" menu already ache, kintu kaje lage na — ekbar order kore fele gele, pore reorder korte hobe seta mone rakhar kono system nei. Chronic-medicine patient (jemon diabetes/hypertension) proti mash manually abar order dey, kokhono kokhono medicine shesh hoye jawar por.
Solution
Per-patient prescription profile — ekbar setup korle, app nijei stock count kore, "Week 1-3 Days Remaining" dekhay, ar refill deadline-er age auto-refill cart ready kore dey. Out-of-stock hole 3-ta shomo bikolpo dekhay (dark pattern na, default selected na).
Ei problem-solution pairing ShareTrip ("Problem 1/Solution") ar Travela ("Problem 01/02")-r moto numbered card format-e case study-te presenting korte hobe.
04 / 20 — Audit Goals & Objectives
Audit Goals & Objectives
Pattern source: Unimart UX Audit-er "Objectives" 2-card format.
  • Goal 1 — Reduce manual reorder effort: Workshop 9 brief-er nijer suggestion onujayi target "4 taps against roughly 14" real-app baseline-er tulonay
  • Goal 2 — Make "days remaining" visible, not just stock count: "Days not Strips" rule — user-ke count na kore, koto din bakche seta directly bolte hobe
  • Goal 3 — No forced auto-charge: "Nothing is charged or shipped without an explicit tap" — trust-preserving confirmation gate
  • Goal 4 — Handle out-of-stock without dark pattern: "Three equal buttons, no default" jokhon originally-prescribed medicine stock-e nei

Heuristic Evaluation

Failure & Redesign Journey (New Section)
Pattern-e kono case study-te explicit "failure" slide nei — sobai polished final dekhay. Kintu real process-e ekta decision-badol-er real example ache tomar kaj-e:
Original decision: Team-er first mid-fidelity wireframe set-e Out-of-Stock/Alternative-Brand flow completely missing chilo — direct "in-stock" assumption kore design kora hoyechilo. Checkout screen-o almost-empty/placeholder-level chilo.
Feedback moment: Content-focused design critique-e (real screen copy vs Workshop 9 brief compare kore) ei gap dhora pore — brief-e explicit "Medicine Out of Stock" flow required chilo, kintu wireframe-e kono screen-i chilo na.
Redesign: "Three equal buttons, no default, no dark pattern" rule diye notun Out-of-Stock screen add kora holo — generic-medicine ar different-brand-same-strength options equal weight-e dekhano hoy, kono ekta pre-selected na.
