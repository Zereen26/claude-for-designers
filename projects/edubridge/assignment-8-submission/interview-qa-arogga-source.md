# Arogga Project: 5 Simple Interview Answers

Real project. Real decisions. Simple English.

---

**1. Tell me about a time you solved a problem with AI.**

I thought Arogga app had 3 missing things: no order confirmation, no order list, no delivery tracking. I used AI to research the real app with 26 real screenshots. It showed me all 3 things already exist in the app. So I rejected my own first idea. The real problem was different: the info exists, but users cannot find it easily.

**2. Walk me through your design process.**

I did not design first. I did research first. I checked real screenshots of the app before drawing anything. That research changed my whole plan. Without it, I would have built features that already existed.

**3. Tell me about a design decision that changed after testing.**

The old order tracker had 8 steps. I tested it. It felt too long and made the wait feel stressful. I cut it down to 5 clear steps. Simple was better than detailed here.

**4. Describe a time you noticed something AI did not catch.**

Arogga's tracker had one big step called "Delivering" for everything after packing. But this is exactly the part users worry about most, is the courier close or not. AI did not flag this. I found it myself from the research. I added a new step, "Out for Delivery," so this moment gets its own alert.

**5. What makes you different from other designers who use AI?**

My team picked the problem together first, as a group decision. Then I asked Claude to do the secondary research. Claude did it very fast and very organized. I already know how to do research myself. But Claude saved me a lot of time, and the work came out cleaner than most people's. That part is real and I am not shy about it.

But I also learned where Claude stops. When I ask Claude to find the real problem, it thinks about it and gives an answer in its own way. There is no real empathy in that answer. It cannot feel what a real user feels. So I went and did real user interviews myself. That is where I saw the real pain points, the ones AI could never find on its own. A real human's real problem only comes out through a real human conversation, not from AI reasoning alone. That is the part AI could not do for me. Only I could do it.

**6. Tell me about a decision AI could not make for you.**

I needed images for product categories. AI could generate fake product photos easily. I said no to that. Fake photos are not honest to users. I used simple color blocks instead and marked them clearly as placeholder, until real photos are added later.

---

Note: research and planning used AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude. Screens were built in Claude Code. But the decisions above, what to keep, what to reject, what to test, were mine.
