# Reference Marketing Post Structures

Onno student/mentor-der real post dekhe **shudhu structure/technique** note kora hoyeche, kono actual line/content na (copyright respect kore, idea nite, copy na kore)। Notun post banar shomoy ei pattern-gulo theke inspiration nite hobe, exact wording kokhono na।

## Pattern 1 — "Interview Session (Part X)" reveal-format
**Source style:** Doctor-appointment/hospital-waiting-time case study post

**Structure:**
1. Crossed-out WRONG initial assumption as title (e.g. ~~Problem = Long Waiting Time~~)
2. Subtitle: "কিছু Interview, কিছু সহজ উত্তর, আর অনেকগুলো নতুন উপলব্ধি | Interview Session (Part X)"
3. "আমরা ভেবেছিলাম X হলে ভালো হবে" — initial hypothesis stated plainly
4. Multiple real interview snippets, labeled by who's speaking (patient, elderly bystander, staff/compounder) — 2-3 different voices building one picture
5. Explicit reframe: "Problem = X" (crossed out) → "Problem = Y" (bolded reveal)
6. The single most powerful quote highlighted as "আমাদের research-এর সবচেয়ে বড় Insight"
7. A generalized UX lesson line (assumption vs. real solution)
8. Engagement question directly tied to the theme
9. Thank you + hashtags

**Note:** Ei structure-i amra "already-existing-feature-discovery" post-e ekbar use korechilam, kintu crossed-out-title/labeled-quote gimmick-ta shoriye personal-confession-style-e likhechilam jate copy na lage। Future-e abar ei "multiple-voice-building-to-one-reveal" technique reuse kora jete pare, notun scene-e।

## Pattern 2 — Infographic + stat card ("Real People. Real Stories. Real Insights.")
**Source style:** Medicine adherence interview post by another UX designer

**Structure:**
- Bold 3-line headline: "Real People. Real Stories. Real Insights."
- Sub-label: "User Interview — [topic]"
- Row of small icon-tags naming the specific features/areas covered (Reminders / Adherence / Refill)
- A stat visualization card: one big % number + a small breakdown bar chart (e.g. Forget/Delay vs Busy Schedule vs Other)
- A real photo of two people talking, with a speech-bubble quote overlaid near the speaker's head
- Bottom banner: one-line mission statement ("Designing solutions that support X")

**Technique to reuse:** stat-card + photo-with-speech-bubble combo is strong for image-prompt work (already used this for the pharmacy/grandmother image prompts).

## Pattern 3 — "1 Week, Many Lessons" retrospective infographic
**Source style:** ArghyaRaj Niloy's Case Study Club post, "Good Designer হওয়ার আগে Good Listener হওয়া জরুরি"

**Structure:**
- Long-form LinkedIn text post (not just image caption) — full narrative essay
- Opens with an honest admission of prior assumption about what a good case study needs
- States a philosophy line early ("Assumption আর Reality কখনোই এক জিনিস নয়")
- Names the specific project/case-study topic and the specific reframe moment (waiting time → uncertainty)
- "What I learned" as a short ✅-marked checklist, phrased as verbs (শুনতে, Challenge করতে, বসে থাকা)
- Explicit "I used to think X, now I think Y" identity-shift statement near the end
- Thank-you naming mentors specifically
- Closing rhetorical question inviting comments
- Companion infographic image: title + "Before/After mindset" 2-column comparison + icon-based "what I learned in 1 week" horizontal step row + a highlighted "biggest takeaway" quote box

**Note:** Amader "Week 2 Marketing Task" post ei pattern follow kore banano hoyeche, kintu prothom draft-e ekta checklist-point near-verbatim copy hoye giyechilo, seta dhora pore fix kora hoyeche। Lesson: structure nite paro, kintu protyekta bullet/line nijer bhasha-y fresh likhte hobe.

## Pattern 4 — Concrete curriculum-report style (mentor's approved sample)
**Source style:** "গত এক সপ্তাহে Case Study Club BD থেকে আমার শেখা ও উপলব্ধি"

**Structure:** (এখন `skills/marketing.md`-এ full detail সহ codified আছে)
- Plain descriptive title, no clever hook needed
- Before/after ধারণা, concrete না abstract
- **Named, specific list of actual course-steps covered** (Design System Explanation, Project Scoping, Research Planning, Survey Question, Interview Question, Field Research, User Interview, Affinity Diagram, Persona, Journey Map)
- A real, specific mentor-feedback anecdote (not generic "mentors are helpful")
- ✅ checklist grounded in the above named steps
- Collaboration point tied to specific interactions (mentor feedback + team discussion)
- Forward-looking line naming actual next course modules (Ideation, MVP, Wireframe, UI Design, Prototype)
- Full thank-you list (all named mentors + "সকল Mentor, Support Mentor")
- Short warm closing + brand-color emoji
- `#CaseStudyClubBD` hashtag (with BD)

**This is now the primary template for weekly retrospective/marketing posts.** Other patterns (1-3) are supplementary technique sources for special-occasion posts (interview stories, infographics), not the default weekly format.
